SZR: auction or market?

What you pay for 1 SZR in the protocol auction and in the Uniswap v4 pool, for the size you want to buy. The numbers are read straight from the Robinhood Chain contracts: the auction price from the Core contract, the market price and depth from the Uniswap v4 SZR/WETH pool (1% fee, one liquidity band). It reads the chain every 5 seconds while the page is open, with no server in between. The money-flow sections add up every event since the protocol started and update every 15 seconds.

How this is estimated

  • Market cost uses the pool's liquidity band, the fee you enter, and the price move your buy causes. It ignores gas and any other liquidity that joins the pool.
  • Auction cost is the quoted price plus the slippage you enter, because the auction curve moves with each buy like a Uniswap V2 pool. Its price decays toward 1.5 times the floor over two days of sale time.
  • "Break-even size" is the buy size where both cost the same. Below it the market is cheaper, above it the auction is cheaper.
